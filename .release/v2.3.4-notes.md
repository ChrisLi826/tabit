## v2.3.4 · Sesame Oil Chicken + Egg Roll

Tabit Chat keeps your question in sight while you scroll up a long reply.

### New
- **What the reply answers.** Scrolled up in a long reply, your message
  that the replies at the top of the view answer shows top right. Scroll
  past another of your messages and it shows that one.
  - Tap it to go to your message.
  - It goes when that message is in view, or at the bottom of the chat.
  - Groups too. Only your own messages show, not what another chat passed on.
