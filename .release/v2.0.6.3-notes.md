## v2.0.6.3 · Danzai Noodles + Hot Grass Jelly

A hotfix: tables on the Remote page line up like they do in tabit.

### Fixed
- **Tables and boxes line up on the Remote page.** The browser drew
  Chinese, box and emoji characters with fallback fonts whose widths do not
  match terminal cells, and Chrome squeezed Chinese punctuation together, so
  a table's borders drifted out of line. Each such character now holds
  exactly its cells: two for Chinese, one for the rest.

### Notes
- Reload the Remote page to get the fix; tabit itself does not need a restart.
