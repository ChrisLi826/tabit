## v2.3.2 · Sesame Oil Chicken + Tofu Pudding

Tabit Chat now offers Claude's guess at your next message, as the tab does.

### New
- **Claude's guess at your next message.** After a reply, Claude Code may
  show a faint guess in the tab's message box, such as "commit it". The
  chat now shows it faint in its empty box too.
  - On a computer, **Tab** puts it in. On a phone, tap the faint text.
    Then send it, or change it first.
  - It shows while the chat is open, about 2 seconds after the tab shows it,
    and goes when the tab starts working.
  - Text typed in the tab itself is never shown as a guess.

### Notes
- Only for Claude tabs in tmux, which is how tabit opens AI tabs unless the
  tmux setting is off: this terminal cannot tell which letters are faint.
- It needs typing on (**Settings… → Remote**), like the tab's screen.
- Restart tabit (or let the update do it), then refresh the chat page.
