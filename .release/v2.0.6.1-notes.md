## v2.0.6.1 · Danzai Noodles + Hot Grass Jelly

A hotfix: Claude tabs keep and come back to the right conversation.

### Fixed
- **A Claude tab comes back to the conversation it is in.** `/clear`,
  `/resume` and a plan's "clear context" give Claude another session id,
  but after a restart the tab resumed the old one and showed an older
  conversation. It now follows the new id.
- **Claude in a tab saves its conversation again when tabit was started
  from a Claude terminal.** Such a tabit passed Claude's own session
  markers to every tab, and Claude there saved no transcript ("Transcript
  saving is off - inherited CLAUDE_CODE_CHILD_SESSION marker"), so the
  conversation could not be resumed or listed. tabit now drops them.
- + AI warns before resuming a session that is already open in another
  terminal: two Claudes writing one session fork its history.

### Notes
- A Claude tab opened before v2.0.3 has no hooks and does not follow a
  new id: open it again.
