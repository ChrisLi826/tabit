## v2.0.6.5 · Danzai Noodles + Hot Grass Jelly

A hotfix: the Remote page's message box is easy to make taller.

### Changed
- **Drag the bar above the message box to change its height.** The
  browser's corner handle sat at the bottom right and pulled the wrong way
  for a box at the bottom of the screen. Drag the bar up to grow the box
  and down to shrink it, with a mouse or a finger, up to 60% of the screen.
  The 📎 and Send buttons stay at the bottom.

### Notes
- The height is kept in that browser. Double-click the bar to reset it.
- Reload the Remote page to get it; tabit itself does not need a restart.
