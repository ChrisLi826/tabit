## v2.0.6.2 · Danzai Noodles + Hot Grass Jelly

A hotfix: the Remote page scrolls up a plain tab too.

### Fixed
- **Scroll up a plain tab's history on the Remote page.** Scrolling a
  tab's screen to the top loaded more lines only for tmux tabs; a tab
  outside tmux stopped at its last 60 lines. It now loads 200 more at a
  time from the tab's own scrollback, up to 3000, until that runs out.

### Notes
- In a plain tab the older lines come without colours, so reading them
  never holds up tabit; the last 60 keep theirs. A tmux tab keeps its
  colours all the way up.
