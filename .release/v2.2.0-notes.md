## v2.2.0 · Ginger Duck + Oolong Tea

Tabit Chat: talk to Claude on this computer from your phone, as a chat.

### New
- **Tabit Chat** at `https://<this-host>.<tailnet>.ts.net/chat` (💬 on the
  Remote page). Each chat is one Claude working in a folder you pick, with a
  model and a mode you pick. Replies stream in as chat bubbles; when Claude
  wants to run a command or change a file, the chat shows a card to allow
  or deny it. It needs "Allow typing into tabs" on.
- **The main chat**, *Tabit Chat*, is always there: bring it anything.
- **Groups** put several chats in one room; they can pass `@name` messages
  to each other, at most 8 in a row before they wait for you.
- **Replies**: tap a message and reply to it; Claude gets the quote.
- **Pictures**: every chat has an animal and a colour you can change.
- **One Claude login for all chats**, switched with 🔑 when there is more
  than one account. Chats go on where they were.
- **Mirror a Claude tab**: a chat shows a tab's conversation and types into
  it. When the tab asks you to pick from a list, the phone shows the
  question as a card you can tap.
- **The AI tabs from a chat**: `tabit-tabs` lets a chat list your AI tabs,
  read one, and type into one, after you tap Allow on a "Send to tab" card.
- **New folder** in the folder picker.

### Notes
- Chats and their history stay on this computer, in
  `~/.local/share/tabit/chats/`. Each chat is a normal Claude session.
- The "Send to tab" card guards against Claude's mistakes; it is not a lock.
  A chat in Bypass mode can do anything you can do on this computer.
- See `docs/chat.md` for the whole guide.
