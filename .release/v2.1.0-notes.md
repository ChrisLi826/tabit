## v2.1.0 · Three Cup Chicken + Wheel Cake

Several Claude accounts at once, a tab that turns from one account to
another, and a phone that can do more of what the desk does.

### New
- **Claude accounts.** Settings > AI adds an account; it logs in once,
  and several stay logged in together. +AI picks the account for each
  Claude tab. Settings, CLAUDE.md, skills, agents and the session list are
  shared, so one list shows every session.
- **Switch a tab's account.** The tab's rename popover (or the tab ⋯ on
  the phone) moves a Claude tab to another account: claude restarts in the
  same tab and goes on with the same conversation. Not while Claude is
  working or asking something.
- **Which account a tab uses.** With two or more logins on the computer,
  every Claude tab's second line says its account, like `@Default · ~/proj`.
- **Settings from the phone.** ⚙ on the Remote page: the AI defaults,
  Claude accounts (add one and log in from the phone: open the link, paste
  the code back), check for updates and update, and restart tabit. It needs
  "typing from the phone" on; Remote's own switches stay at the computer.
- **Remote New tab** browses folders, names the tab, and opens Serial and
  Connect tabs too. A group's ⋯ opens a tab in that group.
- **Remote keeps each tab's unsent message** until you send it or close
  the tab.
- **Remote fits an agent's tmux pane to the phone** while you look at it,
  and gives it back its size about 45 seconds after you leave.
- **Grok tabs** report their status through Grok's hooks, and start in
  minimal mode, so the phone can scroll up through what they said.

### Fixed
- "65;6800;1c" no longer gets typed into tmux tabs: tmux's terminal-id
  questions are dropped on the way.
- An idle Grok logo no longer reads as working.
- A Claude tab in fullscreen mode no longer shows "+2000 lines above" on
  the phone with nothing there to scroll to.
- Connect lists its device types from connect.py.

### Notes
- An update or a restart from the phone waits while a note has unsaved
  changes, and an update needs sudo without a password.
- A new account asks once per folder whether to trust it.
- A Claude tab in fullscreen mode (`/tui fullscreen`) keeps its old lines
  inside Claude, so the phone cannot scroll them. `/tui default` brings
  them back.
