## v2.3.1 · Sesame Oil Chicken + Brown Sugar Cake

Remote now opens in this computer's own browser without Tailscale, both
pages get a text size, and the phone can close a note with unsaved changes.

### New
- **Remote on this computer, without Tailscale.** While Remote is on, tabit
  also serves its pages at `http://127.0.0.1:47810/`, whatever Tailscale is
  doing. **Settings… → Remote** shows the link, with a copy button.
  - The link carries a key. The first open turns it into a browser cookie
    and drops it from the address, so a bookmark keeps working, also after
    a restart.
  - Without the key, other programs and web pages on this computer get 403.
    Other computers cannot reach the port at all.
- **Text size.** ⚙ Settings on the chat and terminal pages starts with
  **Text size**: S, M, L, XL, XXL. It applies at once and each browser keeps
  its own. Icons and pictures keep their size, and the terminal screen keeps
  its own A− / A+.
- **Close a note with unsaved changes from the phone.** Its ⋯ → Close asks
  once more. tabit then keeps the text in
  `~/.local/share/tabit/closed-notes/` and closes the note. If that copy
  cannot be written, the note stays open.

### Changed
- The terminal view's ⚙ shows even with typing off, so the text size can
  always be set. The rest of Settings still needs typing on.
- A restart blocked by an unsaved note now says it can be closed from the
  page too.

### Notes
- Restart tabit (or let the update do it), then refresh the pages.
- The key is kept in `~/.config/tabit/remote-local-key`. Delete it and
  restart tabit to get a new one; old links and cookies then stop working.
