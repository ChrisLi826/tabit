## v2.3.0 · Sesame Oil Chicken + Gua Bao

Tabit Chat can now pass text and files to a colleague's tabit on the same
LAN, like AirDrop, and a new chat can go on with a past conversation.

### New
- **📡 Nearby.** Send text and files (25 MB in all) to another tabit on
  the same office LAN, and get them from one.
  - **Receiving is off** by default and after every start. Turn it on for
    10 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours; it turns itself off when the time is up.
    Sending works while it is off.
  - **Every offer asks first**: who sends, the text, the file names and
    sizes, then **Accept** or **Decline**. Not answered in 2 minutes, it is
    dropped. Accepted files go to `~/.local/share/tabit/uploads/<date>/`.
  - **Put it in** a chat you pick when you accept: its AI gets the text
    and the files' paths. The sender never sees your chats.
  - **Firewall.** With ufw on, tabit opens TCP port 47820 while receiving
    and closes it after (through `sudo -n`; when sudo asks for a password,
    the sheet shows the command to run once). Peers are found with mDNS
    through avahi, which ufw lets through.
- **New chat goes on with a past conversation.** **Conversation** lists the
  folder's past Claude, Codex or Grok conversations that no tab holds,
  newest first; the new tab goes on with the one picked.
- **New chat offers Grok.**

### Fixed
- **Zombies after a restart.** Restarting tabit in place left everything
  the old tabit had started as a zombie, about 50 per restart (1783 were
  seen). tabit now clears them at start and every minute.
- **A group's working bubble** showed a black ring instead of three dots.
- **A Grok or Codex tab's open conversation** now counts as taken, so it is
  not offered again.

### Notes
- Restart tabit (or let the update do it), then refresh the chat page.
- Nearby has **no encryption and no check of who sends**: anyone on the
  LAN can offer you something, and you decide. Its LAN server answers only
  `POST /drop` from private addresses and reaches nothing else of tabit.
- Two tabits on two different computers were not tried yet: the tests ran
  two on one machine, with real avahi. Both sides need this version.
- The Codex list reads the first 8 KB of each rollout; Claude's and Codex's
  conversations are shared by all their logins.
