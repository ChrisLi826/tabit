## v2.3.0.1 · Sesame Oil Chicken + Gua Bao

A hotfix: Claude's progress notes now show in Tabit Chat, and the
background spin turns smoothly.

### Fixed
- **Claude's progress notes were missing from the chat.** While it works,
  Claude writes short notes for you, such as "the test did not start; it
  runs again, results in 8 minutes". The terminal shows them with a
  `· summary` mark. Claude Code saves them as thinking, and Tabit Chat hid
  all thinking, so they never reached the chat. Now they show as Claude's
  messages. Real thinking stays hidden.
  - In a group, a member's notes stay in its own chat. They are not shown
    in the group or passed to other members.
- **The background spin jumped.** The chat list is redrawn every 5
  seconds, and each redraw started the ↻ spin over, so it jumped about
  every third turn. It now keeps turning.

### Notes
- Restart tabit (or let the update do it), then refresh the chat page.
