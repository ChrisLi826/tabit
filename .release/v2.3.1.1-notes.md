## v2.3.1.1 · Sesame Oil Chicken + Brown Sugar Cake

A hotfix: Remote sends about a third of the bytes, so a phone shows a chat
sooner.

### Fixed
- **A chat took 2–3 seconds to show on a phone.** A phone that Tailscale
  cannot reach directly goes through one of its relays, which is slow per
  byte, and opening a chat sent about 280 KB at once. Pages, lists and the
  live updates are now compressed (gzip) when the browser can read it,
  which every current browser can. Each update is still sent the moment it
  happens.
  - Opening a chat: about 280 KB → 78 KB.
  - The chat page: 119 KB → 36 KB.
  - Files opened from the page (images, documents) are sent as they are.

### Notes
- Restart tabit (or let the update do it), then refresh the pages.
- To see whether a phone goes through a relay, run `tailscale status` on
  the computer: `relay "hkg"` means a relay, `direct` means none. Being on
  the same Wi-Fi is not enough when the computer is on another network
  with its own internet line.
