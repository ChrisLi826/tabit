## v2.4.0 · Railway Bento + Brown Sugar Ginger Tea

Tabit Chat gets a Planner: your own to-dos and dates, with an AI chat that
keeps them. Antigravity tabs come into Tabit Chat too.

### New
- **Planner** (the 📅 line above Chats and Groups). It says how many things
  are due today, how many are late, and what is next; tap it to open the
  Planner.
  - Everything lives in one Markdown file, `~/.local/share/tabit/planner.md`,
    that you and an AI can both edit. An item can have a date and time, a
    priority (Urgent, Important, Medium, Low), the AI tabs it is assigned
    to, a description and comments. Ticking it keeps the day it was done.
  - The page shows Today, Coming up, To-do by priority, On hold, and Done
    for the last days you choose. Tap an item to open its card in place;
    the round ＋ adds one.
  - A change names the item and its lines as the page saw them, so an edit
    the AI made meanwhile is never overwritten.
- **The Planner's own chat.** The round 💬 at the lower right starts it the
  first time and opens it in a box over the Planner, with its state bubble.
  It stays out of the chat list and cannot be deleted; ⚙ sets its model and
  thinking effort.
  - Every change you make on the page is told to it in one line, and a
    comment also goes to the tabs the item is assigned to.
  - If its AI ends, the next line starts it again and waits for it. If its
    tab is closed, a new one is made.
- **Antigravity in Tabit Chat.** An Antigravity tab shows as a chat, with
  its replies, tools, status and usage; its model and effort can be set
  and switched.
- **New chat picks the model and the thinking effort**, not only the AI and
  the account.
- **Question cards**: multi-select questions come as cards too, and a
  question's option previews show on the card.
- **Photos from an iPhone upload more reliably**: big photos are made
  smaller first, and an upload that was cut off is tried again.
- **Settings sheets** on both pages look like the Planner's boxes: in the
  middle on a wide screen, from the bottom on a phone.
- **People** in the picture picker (a butler, a desk, an assistant), with
  skin tones from light to dark.

### Changes
- **Skip** on a numbered menu without a No presses Esc.
- The 📡 Nearby button is hidden for now; Nearby itself still works.

### Notes
- Restart tabit (or let the update do it).
- The first time the Planner's chat starts, Claude asks you to trust its
  folder, `~/.local/share/tabit/planner-chat`. Answer it in the 💬 box.
