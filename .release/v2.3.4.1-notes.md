## v2.3.4.1 · Sesame Oil Chicken + Egg Roll

A hotfix for the new top-right message in Tabit Chat.

### Fixed
- **No message showed top right far up a long chat.** A chat loads only
  its latest part; when the replies at the top answered a message older
  than that, nothing showed. The page now looks it up in the history (up
  to 10 pages back, without drawing them) and shows it. A tap loads the
  chat up to it and goes there.

### Notes
- Refresh the chat page. A restart is only needed for the version number.
