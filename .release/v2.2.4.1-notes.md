## v2.2.4.1 · Ginger Duck + Scallion Pancake

A hotfix for groups: what you ask one member alone stays with it.

### Fixed
- **A member's private answers stay out of the group.** A chat in a group
  showed in the group every answer it gave, also to what you asked it in
  its own chat or typed in its tab at the desk, and an `@name` in such an
  answer was passed on to that member. Now only a reply to a message the
  group sent shows in the group. A private answer, its errors and its text
  being written stay in the member's own chat.
- **Asked alone, two tabs in one group talk directly.** When you ask tab A
  in its own chat to ask tab B, A's `@b` line goes straight into B's tab,
  marked `[from @a]`, as tabs talk outside a group. B's answer comes back
  the same way. Neither shows in the group. A reply to the group's own
  message still goes through the group, once.
- A chat with no tab keeps your message before Claude's answer to it. A
  quick answer could be kept first, and then a group took it for an answer
  to the question before.
- The first message a group sends a member now says that its replies to
  these messages show in the group.

### Notes
- Restart tabit (or let the update do it) to get it.
- Only tabs talk directly. A chat with no tab reaches other chats only
  through a group.
- A chat in two groups shows a reply to either group's message in both.
- Two tabs that keep answering each other directly go on until you press
  Stop (Esc) in one of them, as before.
