## v2.2.4.2 · Ginger Duck + Scallion Pancake

A hotfix for Tabit Chat: answers go where their question came from, Stop
presses Esc once, and messages from other tabs look like an AI's.

### Fixed
- **Each answer goes where its question came from.** When you asked a group
  member something in its own chat while it still answered the group (or
  the other way round), the answer could show in the wrong place. Now a
  chat remembers who sent each message, and a tab's chat looks back to the
  message that started the answer. Lines a tab sent on to other tabs, and
  lines you typed at the desk while it worked, no longer change that.
- A line you type in a tab that starts with brackets, like `[WIP]: ...`, is
  no longer taken for a group message.
- After Claude crashed and you sent again quickly, a private answer could go
  to the group, or the chat could stay at "working". Fixed.
- A private "Not sent" error no longer shows in the group.
- Asked alone to call `@name`, a member reaches the group member of that
  name, not another tab with the same name.
- **Stop presses Esc once.** Tapping Stop many times pressed Esc many times,
  and a second Esc in Claude Code opens its Rewind screen, which held every
  new message. Now Stop works only while the tab works or asks, and more
  taps in the next 5 seconds do nothing.
- **Messages from other tabs show as theirs.** A message another AI tab sent
  (`[from @name]`) showed as one you typed. It now shows on the AI side,
  under that tab's picture and name.

### Notes
- Restart tabit (or let the update do it) to get it. The new look for
  messages from other tabs only needs the chat page reloaded.
- A Codex tab in a group: a line you type at the desk while it answers the
  group may still keep that answer out of the group.
